## What is FLL?

**FIRST LEGO League** (FLL, now often "FLL Challenge") is FIRST's LEGO-based
program for younger students (typically ages 9–16, with a junior FLL Discover
for even younger). Teams of ~2–10 students build a robot from **LEGO SPIKE Prime**
(or the older EV3) and complete missions on a themed tabletop mat.

## The FLL Structure

- **Robot Game:** a tabletop challenge mat with missions — the robot earns points
  by doing tasks (moving objects, delivering pieces, pressing mechanisms).
- **Innovation Project:** teams research the season's theme and invent a solution,
  then present it to the judges.
- **Core Values:** gracious professionalism, teamwork, and respecting FIRST's values
  are judged too.

## Season Rhythm

| Phase            | Typical Timing          |
|------------------|--------------------------|
| Kickoff          | August (theme reveal).   |
| Build & program  | ~12+ weeks.              |
| Qualifiers       | Nov–Dec local events.    |
| State / nationals| Jan–Feb.                 |
| World Festival   | Houston, spring.         |

## The Robot Table

- Field is a **resin/wooden table** with a game mat.
- Robot starts in **BASE** (a launch zone) and drives out to do missions, then
  usually returns to BASE for re-arming/throwing parts.
- Runs are **2.5 minutes** (the "Execution Period"). All missions happen in RUNS.

## Core Values (Don't Skip These)

Judges can decide your team's result even if the robot does everything. The four
categories teams are scored on:

1. **Innovation Project** — research + creative solution.
2. **Robot Design** — how the robot is designed/built/programmed.
3. **Core Values** — how the *team* works.
4. **Match Performance** — points won on the table.

A team that nails values + a solid robot usually beats a team with a crazy robot
that argues with judges.
